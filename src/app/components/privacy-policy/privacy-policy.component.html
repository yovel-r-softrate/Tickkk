1. Introduction

Welcome to Tickkk Task Management ("we", "our", "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, store, use, and protect your information when you use our web application.

2. Information We Collect

In order to provide our task management platform, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account Details: Email addresses, passwords (securely hashed), and organization associations.
  • Task Content: Titles, descriptions, subtask checklists, priority configurations, deadlines, and project statuses.
  • Workflow Logs: Action and activity logs representing task updates, completions, and edits to support team collaboration.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to maintain the application, specifically to:

  • Authenticate and manage user access to tasks and organization workspaces.
  • Store and synchronise task metadata and logs across your team.
  • Provide intelligent task optimization insights through AI integration.

4. Data Security & Storage

We implement premium industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical security measures. All client passwords are encrypted using strong one-way hashing techniques, and all network communications use standard TLS encryption to prevent unauthorized interception.

AI Component

5. AI Features & Gemini API

Tickkk features a built-in AI Task Optimizer powered by Google Gemini API. When you request AI recommendations or ask the chat assistant for advice:

  • Only relevant task description context is sent to the Gemini API model to generate workflow and optimization summaries.
  • No personal passwords or raw credentials are ever shared with third-party AI interfaces.
  • Your task details are not utilized for model training by third-party APIs.

6. Your Privacy Rights

You retain the right to edit, update, or purge your user account details, tasks, and data logs. If you request account termination, your data is securely deleted from our databases in accordance with standard data retention policies.
